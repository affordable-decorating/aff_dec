Form 12: Originality Attestation

All contributors must complete this form before a commissioned work is accepted into inventory. Print, complete in ink, and return to Commissions. Incomplete forms will not be processed.

A work is original if it has not previously existed in any form. Resemblance to an existing work is grounds for rejection.

Contributor ID
Commission number
Title of work
Date of completion
Number of times the work was looked at before submission
I attest that this work has not previously existed[  ]
Signature

Questions about this form should be directed to Commissions, Ext. 230.
