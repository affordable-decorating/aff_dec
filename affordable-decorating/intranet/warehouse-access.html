Notice to All Staff: Warehouse Access

Effective immediately, staff entering the warehouse must sign the access log at the loading dock. This applies to all staff regardless of department.

Bay 4 is not to be entered after 5:30 pm. Staff who require access to Bay 4 should make arrangements with Facilities during business hours.

Inventory is not to be moved between bays without a work order. Please do not rearrange items for any reason, including appearance.

Thank you for your cooperation.
