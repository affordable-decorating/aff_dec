Affordable Decorating

Archives and Catalog

At a recent unclaimed property auction, a warehouse and office complex was purchased. By whom is not documented on this site, nor is it interesting or relevant.

The complex’s occupant from some time in the mid 1970s until the mid 1990s was Affordable Decorating. A design consultancy firm that supplied high-volume, low-cost decorating to hotels, corporate offices, and municipal buildings.

This website catalogues the contents of this building for primarily archival purposes. Select items from the warehouse are or will be made available for purchase via personal correspondence.

The firm's Intranet is in the process of being recovered for assistance in understanding the purpose of many of the items that were found that do not seem to fit the firm’s mission. The currently available pages can be found at intranet archive and will be updated as further recovery efforts proceed.
