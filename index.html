Affordable Decorating

Archives and Catalog

At a recent unclaimed property auction, a warehouse and office complex was purchased. By whom is not documented on this site, nor is it interesting or relevant.

The complex’s occupant from some time in the mid 1970s until the mid 1990s was Affordable Decorating,a design consultancy firm whose stated mission was "Supply high-volume, low-cost decorating materials to hotels, corporate offices, and municipal buildings." Their specialty was clearly decor, not inspiring mission statements.

This site catalogues the contents of this building for primarily archival purposes as required by a clause in the auction contract that the purchaser (again, not interesting, relevant nor documented) did not notice and that probably explains the suprisinging lack of competing bids at auction.

Select items from the warehouse are or will be made available for purchase via personal correspondence.

The firm's Intranet has been partially recovered for assistance in understanding the purpose of many of the items that were found in the initial warehouse inventory efforts that do not seem to fit the firm’s stated mission. The currently available pages can be found in our intranet archive and will be updated as further recovery proceeds.
